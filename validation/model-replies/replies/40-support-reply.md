# Draft reply to the customer

Here's a response you can send. It acknowledges the problem, explains what happened without blame, and gives a specific next step.

---

**Subject:** Re: Charged twice for the annual plan

Hi Jordan,

Thank you for writing, and I'm sorry about the double charge. That's not what you signed up for, and I understand why it's frustrating.

I looked at your account and found the cause. Your card was billed twice on March 3 because a payment retry ran at the same time as the original charge. It's a bug on our side, not something you did. We're working on a fix, and we don't expect it to affect anyone else.

Here's what I've done:

1. I've refunded the duplicate charge of $199. Your bank should show it within 5 to 10 business days.
2. I've added a note to your account so that we don't retry a payment that's already succeeded.
3. I've extended your plan by one month at no cost, as a small apology.

If the refund hasn't appeared by the end of next week, reply to this message or write to billing@example.com, and someone will look into it right away. Please include your account email so we can find you quickly.

I know you'd rather have it fixed than hear an apology, so I'll follow up personally on Friday to confirm that the refund has gone through.

Best,
Sam
Customer Support

---

## Why I wrote it this way

- **It opens with the fix, not a policy.** Customers want to know that someone is looking at the problem.
- **It names the specific cause.** We're honest about a bug, which builds more trust than a vague "technical issue."
- **It gives dates.** "Within 5 to 10 business days" is something the customer can check.
- **It doesn't over-apologize.** One clear apology is worth more than three.

## Things to check before sending

- Confirm the amount and date against the billing system.
- Make sure the refund has actually been issued, not just requested.
- Check that the extension is something you're authorized to offer.
- If the customer sounds angry, keep it shorter and skip the explanation until they've replied.
