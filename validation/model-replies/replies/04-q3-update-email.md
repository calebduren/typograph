**Subject:** Q3 results and what we're doing about Q4

Hi team,

Q3's results came in ahead of plan on revenue and behind plan on margin. Revenue reached $4.2M against a $3.9M target, but gross margin landed at 61%, down from 65%. Compared with 2025's third quarter, revenue is up 18%, which is the strongest growth we've posted in two years.

**What drove the result**

- Enterprise renewals closed earlier than forecast, pulling roughly $300K into the quarter.
- Support costs rose after we doubled the onboarding team in July.
- Two large customers negotiated discounts that we hadn't budgeted.

**What we're doing in Q4**

1. Finance will re-forecast by Friday, so we don't plan on Q3's timing quirks repeating.
2. Customer Success will review every discount above 15% before it's offered.
3. Ops will report on onboarding hours per account, so we can see whether the added headcount is paying for itself.

I'd like each of you to send me questions before Thursday's 10:30 a.m. review. Questions about the numbers can go to finance@example.com, and anything about staffing can come straight to me.

Thanks,
Priya

A few notes on the draft:
