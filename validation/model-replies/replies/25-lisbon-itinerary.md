# Three days in Lisbon

Lisbon is hilly, so wear good shoes, and don't over-plan. This itinerary keeps each day to one neighborhood.

## Day 1: Alfama and Baixa

- **9:00 a.m.** Take tram 28 early, before the queues. It's touristy, but the route is genuinely good.
- **10:00** Walk up to the São Jorge Castle. Allow about 90 min. The view of the river alone is worth the ticket.
- **1:00 p.m.** Lunch in Alfama. Look for a place with a short menu on a chalkboard.
- **3:30** Wander down to Baixa and the Praça do Comércio, about 1.5 km on foot, mostly downhill.
- **8:00** Dinner with fado in the evening. Book ahead, because the good places are small.

## Day 2: Belém

Take tram 15 or a 20-minute train from Cais do Sodré, about 6 km west.

1. Jerónimos Monastery. Buy tickets online to skip the line.
2. Belém Tower, for the photo. Going inside is optional.
3. The famous custard tarts at Pastéis de Belém. Order two, since they're small, and eat them warm with cinnamon.
4. MAAT, the museum by the river, if you have energy left.

## Day 3: Sintra

Sintra is a day trip, roughly 40 min by train from Rossio. Plan for a lot of uphill walking.

| Site                | Time needed | Note                 |
| ------------------- | ----------- | -------------------- |
| Pena Palace         | 2 hours     | Go first thing       |
| Quinta da Regaleira | 1.5 hours   | Explore the well     |
| Moorish Castle      | 1 hour      | Skip if you're tired |

## Practical tips

- Buy a rechargeable Viva Viagem card at any metro station.
- Many restaurants close between lunch and dinner, so check hours.
- Tap water is safe to drink.
- Pickpockets work the trams, so keep your bag in front of you.

## Booking

Check the official pages for current prices and times, as they change. The Sintra tourism office publishes a schedule at https://www.parquesdesintra.pt/. If your hotel front desk offers to make dinner bookings, take them up on it.
